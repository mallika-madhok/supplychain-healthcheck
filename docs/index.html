Methodology & data sources

        
How this score is built, where the data comes from, and what it cannot claim.


        
What the score can and can't tell you

        
This tool reports how unusual current freight, currency, and sourcing-concentration
        conditions look relative to their own history — it does not forecast your costs
        and it says nothing about any specific supplier, factory, or brand. Public trade data
        only goes down to product category and country of origin.


        
Scoring

        
Three components, each a z-score (standard deviations from that series' own
        historical average). Bands: below 0 = Low, 0–1 = Elevated, above 1 = High —
        provisional thresholds, not yet validated against a backtest.

        
    
          
  • Freight pressure — the NY Fed Global Supply Chain Pressure
          Index, z-scored against its own history since 1998. It is global, so it is the same
          for every visitor.
    • 
          
  • Currency — USD/CAD 30-trading-day level change and 90-day
          volatility, each z-scored against history since 2017 and averaged. The average is then
          multiplied by the share of your purchases invoiced in USD, so a CAD- or
          EUR-invoiced business sees less currency pressure.
    • 
          
  • Origin concentration — the HHI of your country mix
          (sum of squared shares) z-scored against the spread of HHI across all Canadian apparel
          product lines over the latest 12 months (Canada's own history is too stable month to month
          to be a useful yardstick). "Other" is treated as spread across many suppliers and contributes
          nothing to HHI. This is the part of the score that depends most on your profile.
    • 
        

        
The composite is the equal-weighted average of the three. The Currency tab shows the
        two currency signals separately, and the SQL under every chart is the exact query
        that produced it.

Known limitations

        
    
          
  • The 30-day and 90-day currency windows are counted in trading days (days a
          rate was published), not calendar days.
    • 
          
  • A small number of CIMT rows (~0.0001% of total import value) with no country
          recorded are excluded from the concentration calculation.
    • 
          
  • Origin concentration is measured on country of origin as reported in
          customs data, which can differ from where a garment was cut or sewn.
    • 
          
  • Your profile is a hypothetical mix of spend shares, not actual purchases, so the
          origin component reflects concentration only, not supplier-level risk.
    • 
          
  • Z-score thresholds (Low/Elevated/High) are provisional, set before a full
          backtest against Canadian clothing CPI.
    • 
        


        
How this page works

        
This is a static page with no backend. It loads
        DuckDB-Wasm
        and fetches the Parquet files the data pipeline publishes alongside this page,
        then runs the SQL shown under each chart entirely in your
        browser. Your profile (category and country shares) is encoded in the page URL, not
        sent to any server.
