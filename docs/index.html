Methodology & data sources

        
How this score is built, where the data comes from, and what it cannot claim.


        
What the score can and can't tell you

        
This tool reports how unusual current freight, currency, and sourcing-concentration
        conditions look relative to their own history — it does not forecast your costs
        and it says nothing about any specific supplier, factory, or brand. Public trade data
        only goes down to product category and country of origin.


        
Scoring

        
Three components, each a z-score (standard deviations from that series' own
        historical average): freight pressure (NY Fed GSCPI), currency
        exposure (USD/CAD 30-day level change and 90-day volatility, averaged together
        for the composite but shown separately on the Currency tab), and origin
        concentration (HHI of the Canadian import mix for your category). The composite
        is an equal-weighted (1/3 each) average. Bands: below 0 = Low, 0–1 = Elevated,
        above 1 = High — provisional thresholds, not yet validated against a full backtest.

        
Your own sourcing mix's HHI is shown for comparison against the Canadian-market
        benchmark, but is not itself z-scored into the composite — there's no history for
        a hypothetical profile to be unusual relative to. The what-if sliders change this
        comparison number, not the benchmark itself.

Known limitations

        
    
          
  • The 30-day and 90-day currency windows are counted in trading days (days a
          rate was published), not calendar days.
    • 
          
  • A small number of CIMT rows (~0.0001% of total import value) with no country
          recorded are excluded from the concentration calculation.
    • 
          
  • Origin concentration uses HS2 trade chapters (knit / woven / footwear), a
          different breakdown than the finer NAICS manufacturing categories used elsewhere
          in the underlying pipeline — they don't map one-to-one.
    • 
          
  • Z-score thresholds (Low/Elevated/High) are provisional, set before a full
          backtest against Canadian clothing CPI.
    • 
        


        
How this page works

        
This is a static page with no backend. It loads
        DuckDB-Wasm
        and fetches the three Parquet files the data pipeline publishes directly from this
        repository on GitHub, then runs the SQL shown under each chart entirely in your
        browser. Your profile (category and country shares) is encoded in the page URL, not
        sent to any server.
